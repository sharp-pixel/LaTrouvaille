# Fictional Catalog Naming System

All catalog houses and named product lines are fictional. Generic product descriptors such as `silk slip dress`, `leather tote`, or `dress watch` remain literal because they drive query understanding and facets.

## House families

- French maisons use `Maison`, French surnames, or place-inspired names: Maison Aurelle, Maison Bellune, Ardenne, Laurent Velin, and Louis Marceau.
- Italian fashion and jewellery houses use Italianate workshop or family names: Casa Gilda, Bottega Serena, Belladonna, Buonacera, and Valentieri.
- Swiss watchmakers use place, atelier, or chronometry language: Montreval Geneve, Delacour Geneve, Atelier Montfaucon, and Westerlin Chronometry.
- Anglo-American labels use paired surnames or plain English imagery: Porter & Rowe, Winslow & Co., Cooper Hale, Atelier Rowan, and Gilded Finch.
- East Asian designers use short personal-name forms: Lin Wen and Jun Arata.

## Model vocabulary

Models should reinforce the character of their house and remain stable anywhere they appear:

- Maison Aurelle: Cadre Francais watches, Feline bracelet watches, and Lien jewellery.
- Maison Bellune: quilted flap bags, Page bags, gardenia eveningwear, and double-crescent jewellery.
- Ardenne: Berenice structured bags, Elise bags and watches, and equestrian materials such as taurillon leather.
- Montreval Geneve: Celestine dress watches.
- Atelier Montfaucon: Crown Elm integrated-bracelet watches.
- Delacour Geneve: Heritage dress watches.

When adding a product, first reuse an existing house whose category and origin fit. Add a new house only when it fills a distinct style or market tier, and update search rules whenever a named model is used as a query trigger.
